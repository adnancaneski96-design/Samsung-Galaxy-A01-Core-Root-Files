1.Make sure that your bootloader is unlocked (Settings->Developer Options->Enable OEM Unlock->Download Mode->Unlock Bootloader [Note: This will delete your data Please Backup if needed])
2.Boot into download mode (Power off the device and press volume up and volume down at the same time and connect to a usb cable plugged in a computer then press volume up to continue)
3.Make sure Odin recognizes your device otherwise troubleshoot
4.Prepare the pre-patched boot.tar (boot.img) and select it in the "AP" Slot
5.Press "Start" in Odin and wait for the flash to finish
6.Boot up the Device
7.Install Magisk
8.Select "Direct Install" and Press "Let's go"
9.Wait for the process to end
10.Succesful Root (I hope so)
